### 🎨 AuthSys Professional Logo Prompt

**Goal:** A modern, premium, and futuristic logo for an enterprise security authentication platform.
**Constraint:** NO TEXT. ONLY ICON.

---

#### 🚀 Option 1: The "Digital Shield" (Minimalist & Strong)
> **Prompt:** A high-end minimalist logo icon representing digital security and authentication. The icon features a sleek, futuristic shield with integrated abstract geometric circuit patterns. Deep midnight blue and vibrant electric cyan gradients. Glossy metallic finish with subtle glassmorphism effects. Set against a clean, solid dark background. Symmetrical, balanced, professional, corporate identity style. High resolution, 8k, vector style, flat design with depth. No text, no words, icon only.

#### 🔗 Option 2: The "Cyber Vault" (Secure & Technical)
> **Prompt:** A professional geometric logo icon for a cybersecurity platform. The design features a stylized, abstract digital vault or a hexagonal node structure representing connectivity and protection. Glowing neon blue accents on a matte carbon fiber texture. 3D isometric perspective with clean lines. Premium tech aesthetics, cyberpunk minimalism. High contrast, sharp edges. No text, no letters, standalone symbol only.

#### 🔑 Option 3: The "Abstract Key-Node" (Modern & Unique)
> **Prompt:** A modern abstract logo symbol for a software authentication system. The design combines a stylized key silhouette with digital network nodes. A continuous line art style with a sense of flow and logic. Vibrant HSL blue and violet gradients. Futuristic, sleek, and high-tech. Perfect for a software dashboard icon. Flat vector design, white space usage, premium branding. No text, no labels, just the icon.

---

### 💡 Tips for Generation:
*   Use **DALL-E 3** or **Midjourney** for best results.
*   If using Midjourney, add `--no text words letters` to the end of the prompt.
*   Focus on the **Gradients** and **Geometric** terms to match your dashboard's premium feel.
